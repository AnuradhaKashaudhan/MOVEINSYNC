Core Finding: The Problem is the 1-Star Tail
MoveInSync maintains an aggregate rating of 4.64 / 5.0 because 85.5% of commuters rate 5 stars for routine, uninterrupted commutes. However, ratings do not decay into mild 3-star dissatisfaction—they polarize immediately into 1-star ratings (7,324 reviews) when acute operational failure modes strike. Commuters stranded late at night, locked out by broken OTP fields during shift cutoffs, or penalized with salary deductions experience catastrophic frustration. Addressing the top 3 volume drivers yields an expected rating lift of +0.06 to +0.09 stars (upper bound: ~4.88 if all 1-2★ ratings became 4 stars).

Methodological Assumptions & Limitations
  • Sampling Rule: Analysis is strictly conducted on the 24-month recent window (Oct 2024 to Oct 2026). The 9,205 legacy archive reviews were excluded to prevent historical app versions from skewing current engineering priorities.
  • Sample Independence: The 1-2 star group (1,671 rows) represents 100% of available scraped low-star reviews. The 3-5 star group (1,500 rows) is a random sample and is never combined with the low-star group to avoid artificial base-rate distortions.
  • Near-Duplicate Reviews: Scanning reviews with at least 15 words and cosine similarity > 0.85 yielded 0 pairs (possible templated text, not verified).
  • Model Validation: Independent evaluation on n=70 held-out reviews (never shown to model) demonstrated a 98.6% theme match rate and a 77.5% macro F1 score across active themes using gemini-3.5-flash-lite.
  • Causal Attribution: Version coincidences reflect observed statistical correlation in review counts and release dates. External hypotheses are labeled hypothesis, not verified unless confirmed by internal telemetry.
  • Validation Limits: Evaluated on n=70 held-out reviews labeled by a single annotator. Seed reviews are longer than typical reviews. Weak F1 was observed for DRIVER_CONTACT (50%) and ROUTING_STOPS (44%), so those counts are probably undercounted. NO_SHOW precision is 50% in the multi-label view.
